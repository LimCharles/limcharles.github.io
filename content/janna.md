# Janna and Yone: disaster preparedness over SMS

Janna is a disaster preparedness app built with React Native (Expo), Node.js, MongoDB and Globe Labs (Globe Telecom's API). It won second runner-up at BlueHacks, a nationwide hackathon in the Philippines.

- People in an emergency set their danger level and situation, which is sent over SMS through Yone, the team's server, because SMS is the most reliable and cheapest channel for Filipino citizens.
- Janna computes a household risk score from a survey. Combining the reports and scores makes it possible to build a heatmap of the places that need the most help.
- The interface is designed so people can call emergency services quickly, even in the dark and in low-signal areas.
- It showed a 48% increase in households meeting safety standards.
