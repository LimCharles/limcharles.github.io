# About Charles Lim

Charles Lim is an AI engineer based in Singapore.

He has a Master of Computing in Artificial Intelligence from the National University of Singapore (NUS), where he graduated as valedictorian, and a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Ateneo de Manila University with magna cum laude standing. Before NUS he was an AI engineer at Equitec and the founder and CTO of WayKo, a logistics startup. Details are in the resume and experience documents.

His recent work is mostly about LLM systems: memory for language models (vahumana), agents that act on their own (Laile's coach, Lele), and multi-agent pipelines (Haitham). This site's chat is itself a small retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) setup.

Charles describes himself as a good and fun person. He loves rum raisin and bubblegum ice cream.
