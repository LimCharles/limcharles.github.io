# DeliveryEase

DeliveryEase (2023) was built by Charles's team (Group 1) at the GDSC-L hackathon. It tackles logistics problems in the Philippines with Sustainable Development Goal 7 in mind: it optimizes delivery routes to cut distance travelled, fuel use and pollution.

Built with Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, Firebase (serverless backend and real-time updates) and the Google Maps API. It is a prototype, not deployed for production.

Code: https://github.com/LimCharles/DeliverEase
